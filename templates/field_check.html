BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Good decisions start with good field data.

Recent and accurate measurements help you understand what the advisory can tell you about your crop.

  • Use recent soil moisture and rainfall readings.
  • Use a soil test for nutrient and pH values where available.
  • Check local weather before deciding to irrigate.
  • Inspect plants and confirm suspected disease with an expert.
Important to know
This version does not connect to live sensors, a weather API, or image-based disease diagnosis. Model outputs are estimates, not confirmed field measurements or diagnoses.
