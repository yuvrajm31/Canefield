Irrigation engine

From a moisture reading to a pumping plan.

Canefield compares your soil moisture with the target for the crop stage, converts the gap into millimetres of water over your root zone, adjusts for how efficient your irrigation method is, and scales it to your acreage.

deficit = (target − moisture) ÷ target
depth  = deficit × root zone ÷ efficiency
volume = depth × acres → kL → pump hours

Early-warning alerts

Small problems, flagged before they grow.

Acidic or alkaline soil, waterlogged fields, missing readings and high disease-screening risk all surface as alerts with a plain-language reason and a suggested next step.

pH < 5.5 → liming alert
pH > 8.0 → gypsum / organic matter alert
moisture > target + 10 → hold irrigation
humidity ≥ 82% & temp ≥ 28°C → high disease risk

Good to know

Straight answers.

Does it use live sensors or weather?

Not yet. Canefield works from the readings you enter and the research dataset. Update your farm's soil-moisture value and re-check to get a fresh plan.

How accurate are the quantities?

They are guideline estimates based on standard rates, root-zone depth and irrigation efficiency. Treat them as a starting point and verify with a soil test and local agricultural advice.

What happens when I mark an irrigation as done?

The action moves to your history and the farm's soil moisture is reset to its stage target, so the next re-check reflects the water you applied.

Is my farm data private?

Yes. Each farmer has a separate account, and farms, recommendations and advisories are stored against that account in the database.
